I'm Jeppe. I live in Odense, Denmark, where I design stuff.

I am a third-semester Bachelor's student in Robotics at the University of Southern Denmark, Odense. My interests lie at the intersection of electronics, software development, computer vision, and machine learning. I enjoy applying C++ programming and innovative problem-solving to design and develop intelligent robotic systems.

I’ve been fascinated by technology for as long as I can remember, but my passion for robotics really began during high school, where I studied mechatronics. Even before that, I loved computers and creating things from scratch, whether it was tinkering with electronics or writing small programs.

Since starting my Bachelor’s in Robotics at the University of Southern Denmark, I’ve been diving deeper into electronics, software development in C++, computer vision, and machine learning. I’ve worked on several projects that challenged me to combine these skills, from building robotic arms to developing vision systems that can interpret and interact with the environment.

Looking ahead, I aim to continue advancing in machine learning and explore how intelligent systems can be designed to solve real-world problems, pushing the boundaries of what autonomous robots can achieve.
