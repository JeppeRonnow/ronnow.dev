# Semester project in "Autonomous Robots"

**Technologies:** UR5, URrtde, C++, OpenCV, SQlite, QT


Designed and programmed a UR5 robotic arm to identify and sort M&Ms using
machine vision and a custom-built gripper.

![[img/mm.jpg]]

In my second-semester project at the University of Southern Denmark, my team and I designed and built an autonomous robot capable of identifying and sorting M&Ms by color. The system combined a UR5 robotic arm, a custom-built gripper, and a camera-based vision system to detect the candies and place them into color-matched containers.

My role focused on developing the machine vision system, enabling the robot to “see” the M&Ms, recognize their colors, and determine their positions for precise pick-up. The project challenged us to integrate mechanical design, electronics, software, and computer vision into a single, fully automated process.

After several iterations and testing, our system achieved over 94% sorting accuracy in hour-long endurance runs, meeting our performance goals and demonstrating the potential of combining robotics and vision technology for automated manufacturing tasks.
